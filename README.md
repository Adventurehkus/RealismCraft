# RealismCraft

Experience Minecraft survival with deeper realism, immersive environments, and enhanced exploration.

*   **Realistic Survival:** Keep track of thirst, body temperature, stamina, and the changing seasons.
*   **Terrain and Biomes:** Explore towering mountains, dense forests, and expanded biomes.
*   **Immersive Visuals:** Includes atmospheric lighting, realistic water, enhanced animations, as well as tree and item physics.
*   **More to Discover:** Grow crops, prepare new meals, create detailed builds, and experiment with engineering.
*   **Made for Exploration:** Take on a deeper survival experience with new challenges and details.
