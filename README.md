# RealismCraft

Experience Minecraft survival with deeper realism, immersive environments, and enhanced exploration.

- **Realistic Survival:** Manage thirst, temperature, seasons, stamina, and other environmental challenges.
- **Immersive World:** Explore enhanced terrain, expanded biomes, and detailed landscapes.
- **Visual Realism:** Enjoy atmospheric shaders, realistic water, physics, and enhanced animations.
- **Deeper Gameplay:** Discover expanded farming, cooking, engineering, and building possibilities.
- **Built for Exploration:** Experience a more immersive world while balancing realism, performance, and classic Minecraft freedom.

Prepare, explore, build, and survive in a world that feels more alive.
